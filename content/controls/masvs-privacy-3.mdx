## What This Control Means

Tell users what data is collected and shared, including unexpected background collection, and follow platform data-declaration requirements.

## Why This Matters

Users have a right to know how their data is used; hidden collection is both a compliance and a trust failure.

## Main Security Requirement

> The app is transparent about data collection and usage.

## Common Failure Patterns

- Collecting data not covered by the privacy policy.
- Omitting background or third-party collection.
- Skipping platform data-safety declarations.

## Secure Implementation

Maintain an accurate privacy policy, declare data practices per platform guidelines, and notify users of non-obvious collection.

## Key Rules

- Keep privacy declarations accurate and current.
- Declare background and third-party collection.
- Follow platform data-safety forms.

## Checklist for Code Review

- Does the privacy policy match actual collection?
- Is background/third-party use disclosed?
- Are platform data declarations complete?
