## What This Control Means

Use unlinkability techniques such as abstraction, anonymization, and pseudonymization so device IDs and behavioral signals cannot re-identify a user.

## Why This Matters

Persistent identifiers enable cross-app tracking and de-anonymization.

## Main Security Requirement

> The app prevents identification of the user.

## Common Failure Patterns

- Sending raw device IDs and identifiers to analytics.
- Reusing a single pseudonymous id across purposes.
- Fingerprint-like signals shared between vendors.

## Secure Implementation

Avoid advertising IDs where possible, isolate per-purpose pseudonymous identifiers, and apply aggregation or differential-privacy style safeguards.

## Key Rules

- Avoid persistent advertising identifiers.
- Isolate identifiers per purpose.
- Use anonymization and pseudonymization techniques.

## Checklist for Code Review

- Are device IDs minimized or avoided?
- Are identifiers isolated per purpose?
- Is re-identification risk assessed?
